### Non-blocking follow-ups

1. The archive checks require the `.info` file, while the claims check reads generated build-tree metadata. No check compares the extracted archive's `.info` payload to the generated contents. Affects PLAYER-01 and DOCS-01.
2. The runner has an exact Atari 2600 refusal assertion. The libretro consumer uses the shared formatter, but no consumer test captures that frontend log. Affects PLAYER-01 and DOCS-01.
3. The C quickstart says to use source matching the package. It does not give a direct source-at-tag retrieval step, although the checked-in example and archive are paired in package verification. Affects EMBED-01.
4. Phase 30 records pre-existing trailing whitespace in `.planning/REQUIREMENTS.md` and `30-RESEARCH.md`; both were outside its plan-owned files.

These are coverage and usability improvements, not broken runtime flows or unsatisfied requirement outcomes. The archived Phase 30 record also contains one informational TODO marker in a historical research row; it does not represent unfinished product work.
