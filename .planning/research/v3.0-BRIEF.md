# v3.0 milestone research brief

Approved direction, 2026-10-29: Initially States and movies, refined to Playstead-ready play and states with gamer-first Playstead
viability. The owner wants muhtari to be the first end-to-end proof of playing
games in their emulator frontend; save/load states are high priority. Inspect the
local sibling Playstead project and its brand book read-only before assuming the
adapter/product contract. No personal filesystem paths enter tracked artifacts.

Current delivery: integer-only bounded C17 library, headless runner, libretro;
package v0.10.0, API7/ABI1/behavior6, NTSC/PAL, scoped boards/controllers, raw HSC/
EEPROM battery persistence, movie2 replay. No complete state or recording support.
Owner casually played Choplifter and Ninja Golf successfully; these observations
are recorded, not a commercial-library guarantee or recurring owner UAT.

Research only NEW capabilities and the concrete integration delta. Do not reopen
settled decisions without new evidence or design individual implementation plans.
Use fan-out/prompt.txt, AGENTS.md, PROJECT.md, LESSONS.md, relevant preparation docs,
and existing source. Never open GPL/LGPL/unlicensed emulator source. Local frontend
source and its docs are allowed; inspect applicable instructions there first.

Selected seeds: 002 (state/replay/continuation), 007/009/010 (bounded recurring
tune-up with measured pipeline/property cost). Other seeds stay unpromoted unless
the actual approved consumer requires them. Browser008 is the high-priority next
alternative after v3.0; refresh the horizon with rationale/triggers. States supply
RetroArch save/load, rewind/run-ahead and portable resume. Recording/replay serves
real host needs and reproducible verification; AI agents/TAS/speedrun tooling stay
later. No GUI/device code in this repo, no new runtime dependency presumed.

Deliver scoped alternatives, one recommendation, sources/confidence, cheapest
falsifiable acceptance and remaining phase-research questions. Preserve actual
host/battery/firmware ownership, transactional hostile-input rejection, integer
boundedness, no allocation after load and all supported device/region behavior.
Known COST-01/WR-01/advisory-property debt stays explicit; tune-up scope must be
bounded and ship improvements, not become a general audit or new metrics system.

There are four research dimensions: STACK, FEATURES (actual Playstead consumer),
ARCHITECTURE (state and ownership), PITFALLS (adversarial security/evidence). Each
owns one top-level research file and writes concise milestone-level guidance.
Preserved prior top-level research is in milestones/v2.0-research. Root orchestrator
alone writes shared PROJECT/STATE/REQUIREMENTS/ROADMAP and performs commits.

Final scope and accepted priority changes are in ../REQUIREMENTS.md and SUMMARY.md.
