<objective>
Finish SALLY's interrupt rule: NMI taken by φ2 index with no per-cycle cost (D-16 with C5), where the poll point is
the φ2 index at which the instruction's deciding cycle actually completed, so RDY and HALT holds inside an instruction
are counted where they fall.

Purpose: CPU-03's NMI clauses on the CPU side; plan 6 adds the real-bus latch and its WSYNC case.
Output: NMI and the poll mark in cpu.c, six `cpu.nmi.*` cases, the per-vector poll check, the NMI section of
`docs/hardware/sally.md`.

Mechanism (the poll mark). The seed, szTheory/nesturbator @9f9386c661f6 `src/cpu.c` with `src/bus.c`, marks its
interrupt-poll point at the bus seam: `cycle()` overwrites `poll_latch` at the start of every cycle, so the sample the
instruction end sees is the one taken as the final cycle began, which is where the penultimate cycle ended in real
cycles, however long earlier cycles ran. This plan keeps that placement, the boundary right after the deciding cycle,
and moves the store from every cycle in the bus to once per instruction in cpu.c: `m->cpu.poll_phi2 = m->bus.phi2`
right after the deciding cycle's bus call returns. A hold inside or before the deciding cycle raises `m->bus.phi2`
before the store; a hold in a later cycle does not reach it. RESEARCH Pattern 2's `m->bus.phi2 - 1` at instruction
end and an index counted from the start (start + N - 1) are the two wrong models the hold cases rule out.
</objective>
